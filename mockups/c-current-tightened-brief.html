Synopsis

Conversion Lift hides your ads from a random slice of people and compares their buying with everyone who saw them. The gap is what the ads actually caused. Press says Search and PMax can now self-serve it. Entry: 1,000 conversions and a $5,000 budget.

The interesting bits

  1. Google's own help page doesn't back the headline yet — it still says 'contact your account rep'.
  2. Google is admitting attribution isn't the truth. You don't build a tool to measure what ads caused if the normal numbers already told you.
  3. The $5k / 1,000-conversion floor shuts out most small accounts. Big spenders get proof; everyone else keeps trusting the dashboard.
  4. Under 90% certainty it's only 'directional', and short tests under-measure lift by ~17%.
